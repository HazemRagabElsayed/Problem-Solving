Business Requirement

Management wants a report showing each employee's bonus percentage based on both department and salary.

Rules:

IT employees:
Salary >= 10000 → 15%
Otherwise → 10%
Sales employees:
Salary >= 8000 → 12%
Otherwise → 8%
Finance employees:
Always 18%
HR employees:
Always 5%
Expected Output
EmployeeID EmployeeName	Department	Salary   BonusPercentage
1	    Ahmed	 	IT		12000   15
2	    Sara		HR		6500      5
...	    ...		...	...	...

Requirements
Use one CASE expression.
Return the bonus percentage as an integer (e.g., 15, not '15%').
Keep the query clean and production-ready.
