Business Background

An e-commerce company stores customer information in a Customers table.

The business wants to prevent duplicate registrations based on email address.

Existing Table
Customers
---------
CustomerID
FirstName
LastName
Email
Phone
CreatedDate
Requirements

Write a stored procedure that:

Accepts customer information.
Checks whether the email already exists.
If the email exists:
Do not insert the customer.
Return a descriptive message.
Otherwise:
Insert the customer.
Return a success message.
Additional Business Rules
Email comparison should be case-insensitive.
The procedure should be safe for future growth.
Avoid unnecessary table scans.
Write production-quality code.
