Business Requirement

Management wants every order classified into a pricing category.

Rules:

TotalAmount		Category
أقل من 100			Budget
 من 100 إلى أقل من 300		Standard
من 300إلى أقل من 700    	Premium
أكثر من أو تساوي 700		VIP
Expected Output
OrderID	CustomerName	TotalAmount	Category
1	Ahmed		45		Budget
2	Sara		120		Standard
...	...	...	...
Requirements
Use CASE
Give the calculated column the alias:
Category
Write the query as if it were going into a production report.
Keep it clean and readable.
Think like a professional

Before writing your query, ask yourself:

Should I use a Simple CASE or a Searched CASE?
Are the boundary conditions (100, 300, 700) handled correctly?
Is there any possibility of overlapping conditions?
Is the query easy for another developer to understand?
